ABOUT

A charitable trust with a multidisciplinary vision

The Trust Deed describes Sur Sanjeevani Trust as a private charitable trust and sets out objects spanning evidence-based music therapy, interdisciplinary research, education, healthcare, psychological well-being, Indian music traditions, social transformation and community service.

🎵

Music Therapy

Evidence-based music therapy and therapeutic approaches, including interdisciplinary work.

🔬

Research & Innovation

Research projects, centres, documentation, publications, databases and collaborations.

🌱

Community Well-being

Outreach, awareness, supportive services and charitable activities for public benefit.

MUSIC THERAPY

Music as an evidence-informed pathway to well-being

The deed includes evidence-based music therapy and interdisciplinary work involving psychology, social work, medical sciences, health sciences, neuroscience, yoga, music, Indian knowledge systems and education.

  • Preventive, promotive and rehabilitative approaches
  • Therapeutic and supportive interventions
  • Community-based programmes and outreach
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration and research
♫

Holistic approach

Integrating music, science, psychology, healthcare and Indian knowledge traditions for holistic well-being, research, education and social transformation.

RESEARCH & INNOVATION

From ideas to evidence

Interdisciplinary research

The deed provides for research across music therapy and related scientific, health, psychological and educational disciplines.

Centres & laboratories

It provides for laboratories and centres dedicated to music therapy, psychology, behavioural sciences, public health and related disciplines.

Knowledge dissemination

Documentation, publications, databases, indices, libraries, periodicals and educational/scientific materials are included among the stated objects.

EDUCATION & PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Learning that connects practice and knowledge

The Trust Deed provides for diploma, degree and continuing education, faculty development, internships, fellowships, professional training, seminars, conferences, workshops and lectures, including national and international collaboration.

PUBLICATION INITIATIVE

International Journal of Indian Music Therapy

The Trust Deed permits preparation and publication of books, materials, periodicals and other literature required for the Trust's objectives, including educational and scientific publications.

Official journal details such as ISSN, editorial board, submission guidelines and publication schedule should be published only after they are formally confirmed by the Trust.
